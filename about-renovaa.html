About RENOVAA

It started with a simple question

The idea for RENOVAA was born while our founder was completing his Master's degree in Digital Business & Innovation and developing a cosmetics brand of his own.

While building the brand's website and digital customer experience, he noticed a fundamental problem: skincare customers often struggle to understand what their skin needs, which products are right for them and where to begin.

He realised that, as a founder, he would have wanted a tool on his own website that could analyse each customer's skin, understand their individual needs and guide them towards the most suitable products.

The technology he imagined needed to be intelligent, easy to integrate and designed specifically to modernise skincare brands.

So, he decided to build it.

What we do

RENOVAA provides AI-powered skin analysis technology for skincare and cosmetics brands.

Our solution is embedded directly into a brand's website, allowing customers to receive a personalised skin analysis without leaving the online shopping experience.

Through a facial scan and a short questionnaire, RENOVAA identifies visible skin concerns, understands the customer's goals and recommends relevant products or complete routines from the brand's existing catalogue.

The result is a more personalised and engaging shopping journey that helps customers make confident decisions while helping brands increase conversion, retention and customer understanding.

How we do it

RENOVAA combines artificial intelligence, computer vision and customer-provided information to create a personalised skin profile.

The experience begins with a guided facial scan. Our technology analyses different areas of the face to identify visible skin characteristics and concerns. The customer's questionnaire answers then provide additional context about their skin, sensitivities, current routine and personal goals.

RENOVAA connects these insights with the brand's product catalogue to generate personalised product and routine recommendations in real time.

For the brand, the RENOVAA analytics dashboard transforms anonymous customer interactions into actionable customer and market insights. This helps teams better understand their audience, identify emerging skincare needs and improve their product, marketing and commercial strategies.

Our mission

Our mission is to modernise the way skincare brands understand and serve their customers.

We want to replace skincare uncertainty with clarity, helping customers discover what their skin needs while giving brands the intelligence required to create more personalised and effective digital experiences.

From the first analysis to the final product recommendation, RENOVAA brings personalised skin intelligence directly into the online skincare journey.

From skin analysis to cart — inside your brand experience.
